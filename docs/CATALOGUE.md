# The catalogue -- every word the model may commit

> GENERATED by `python3 -m lang.form write` from `shared/catalogue_v1.json`.
> Do not edit this file: edit the catalogue and regenerate. CI fails if they disagree.

The model thinks freely, then commits one decision by filling the form of
[ADR 0005](adr/0005-the-commitment-form.md). Every value in the form is one
of the words below; anything else is refused with a reason. **No field takes
a coordinate.** Every number carries the user's own words, and must appear in
them, in the user's unit.

### Relation parameters

- **`axis`** -- The direction along which two things line up. Values: `horizontal` -- Same height on the sheet: centres on one horizontal line.; `vertical` -- Same position across the sheet: centres on one vertical line.
- **`count`** -- How many copies to distribute. A number (quantity `count`).
- **`distance`** -- How far from the target. A number (quantity `distance`).
- **`side`** -- Which side of the target the object goes on. Values: `above` -- Towards the top of the sheet from the target.; `below` -- Towards the bottom of the sheet from the target.; `inside` -- Towards the inside of the target's outline (a room's interior side of a wall).; `left` -- Towards the left of the sheet from the target.; `outside` -- Towards the outside of the target's outline.; `right` -- Towards the right of the sheet from the target.

## Properties

- **`corner`** -- Which corner of the host a corner feature goes on.
  - `all` -- Every corner of the host.
  - `top_left` -- The top-left corner on the sheet.
  - `top_right` -- The top-right corner on the sheet.
  - `bottom_left` -- The bottom-left corner on the sheet.
  - `bottom_right` -- The bottom-right corner on the sheet.
- **`hinge_side`** -- Which jamb of a door carries the hinges, seen from the side it opens towards.
  - `left` -- Hinges on the left jamb.
  - `right` -- Hinges on the right jamb.
- **`hole_type`** -- Whether a hole goes all the way through its host.
  - `blind` -- Stops at the given depth.
  - `through` -- All the way through the host.
- **`orientation`** -- How a schematic symbol lies on the sheet.
  - `vertical` -- Its terminals top and bottom.
  - `horizontal` -- Its terminals left and right.
- **`pipe_use`** -- What a pipe carries; decides its layer and line style.
  - `gas` -- Gas supply.
  - `sewer` -- Foul sewage.
  - `storm` -- Rainwater drainage.
  - `water` -- Drinking water supply.
- **`swing`** -- Which way a door opens relative to the room it belongs to.
  - `inward` -- Opens into the room.
  - `outward` -- Opens out of the room.
- **`thread`** -- ISO metric coarse thread of a threaded hole.
  - `M3` -- nominal 3 mm
  - `M4` -- nominal 4 mm
  - `M5` -- nominal 5 mm
  - `M6` -- nominal 6 mm
  - `M8` -- nominal 8 mm
  - `M10` -- nominal 10 mm
  - `M12` -- nominal 12 mm
  - `M16` -- nominal 16 mm
  - `M20` -- nominal 20 mm
  - `M24` -- nominal 24 mm
